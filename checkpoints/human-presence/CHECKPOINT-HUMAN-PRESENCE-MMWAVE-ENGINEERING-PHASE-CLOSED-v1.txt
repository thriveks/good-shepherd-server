VALIDATED CAPABILITIES
======================

The Human Presence / mmWave development work established:

1. Normal human-presence sensing remains operational.

2. A separate high-resolution observer-only evidence channel was implemented
   and validated without replacing normal mmWave operation.

3. Approximate local acquisition cadence:
   ~10 samples per second.

4. Approximate development evidence transport cadence:
   one compact batch approximately once per second while explicitly enabled.

5. Runtime controls were implemented and production validated:
   high_res_enable
   high_res_disable

6. High-resolution mode defaults OFF after reboot.

7. Disabling high-resolution mode clears pending development evidence batches.

8. Normal mmWave operation remains available while high-resolution development
   capture is disabled.

9. ESP32 uptime-based sensor-clock reconstruction was validated and removes
   server receipt jitter from temporal sample spacing.

10. Controlled labeled activity testing was completed.

11. Normalized moving-distance temporal shape was supported as the strongest
    evaluated temporal activity feature.

12. Stationary spatial dispersion showed useful repeatability.

13. Posture-transition direction showed repeatability for the tested geometry:
    stand-up generally toward radar
    sit-down generally away from radar

14. Entry/exit directional behavior showed strong repeatability in the tested
    geometry.

15. General walking direction was rejected as a semantic activity classifier.

16. movingTarget was rejected as a semantic walking/activity classifier.

17. stationaryEnergy was rejected as a primary temporal feature because it was
    effectively saturated during the controlled evidence set.

18. radarPresence was rejected as a discriminative feature because it was
    saturated during the controlled evidence set.

19. stationaryTarget was rejected as a discriminative feature because it was
    saturated during the controlled evidence set.

20. targetState did not establish independent discriminative evidence.

21. stablePresenceState did not establish independent discriminative evidence.

22. Adding moving-energy temporal shape to normalized moving-distance temporal
    shape did not improve the v1 evidence separation and was rejected from the
    v1 combination.

FALL-DETECTION BOUNDARY
=======================

Fall detection was NOT established.

The controlled work did not justify:

- a fall classifier
- a fall probability score
- a fall threshold
- an emergency classification
- a medical inference
- automatic emergency escalation based on mmWave evidence
- a claim that LD2410 movingTarget represents semantic human movement
- a claim that distance trajectory alone distinguishes a fall from all
  intentional activities

Intentional lie-down and rise-from-lying tests were completed.

Whole-event direction was not sufficiently repeatable to establish a
fall-detection model.

Temporal moving-distance structure was observed, but distance-only
classification was not supported.

Therefore future Good Shepherd product behavior must not label an mmWave event
as a confirmed fall based on this engineering phase.

PRODUCT-SAFE INTERPRETATION
===========================

The validated mmWave work may support product features concerning:

- presence
- room occupancy
- activity/routine evidence
- stationary-versus-spatially-dispersed behavior
- longitudinal behavioral patterns
- unusual activity observations
- room entry/exit context where supported by broader system evidence
- observer-only research/development activity characterization

Any future customer-facing or operator-facing interpretation must preserve the
difference between:

A. measured sensor evidence
B. behavioral interpretation
C. operational alert policy

No unsupported medical or emergency conclusion should be presented as sensor
fact.
