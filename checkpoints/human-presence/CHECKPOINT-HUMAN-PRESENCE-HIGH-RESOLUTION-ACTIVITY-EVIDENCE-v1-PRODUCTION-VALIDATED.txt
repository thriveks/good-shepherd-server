============================================================
NORMALIZED TEMPORAL SHAPE FINDINGS
============================================================

sit_down:
clearest repeatable normalized moving-distance pattern in the tested set

intentional_rise_from_lying:
consistently showed a low middle trough followed by higher late distance

intentional_lie_down:
also showed a middle trough in multiple trials

Therefore:

A middle distance trough alone cannot distinguish lie-down from rise.

Cross-room absolute centimeter magnitudes must not be treated as
classification proof because generic posture trials and targeted lie/rise
trials were performed with different physical sensor geometry.

Normalized within-event shape is safer than absolute cross-room distance
magnitude for continued analytical work.
